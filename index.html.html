Upload CSV Monthly Report SPX

Unggah file performance_report_*.csv hasil export Monthly dari portal SPX. Sistem otomatis membuang kurir non-aktif (0/0%) dan memetakan 4 kategori armada.

Alur Tarikan Data Monthly Report SPX:
  1. Buka & login ke spx.shopee.co.id/#/dashboard/all-mile-hub/lm
  2. Pilih menu Workforce Management
  3. Pilih Driver Performance Management
  4. Pilih Delivery Performance Report
  5. Ubah periode ke Monthly & pilih Monthly Report terbaru
  6. Klik Search lalu klik Export
